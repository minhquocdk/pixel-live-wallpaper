Island, alive

A painted island, brought to life: the river ripples, the falls pour, the grass catches the wind, thirty-three crystals glow with beats of their own, and the hour turns from noon through dusk to night. The art stays the art — only what moves is painted over it — so this is the shape a live wallpaper takes.

What moves
frame– paint · blit– fps– pixels that move– of the picture–

The still picture is graded once each time the light changes; a frame is that picture copied and the pixels that move written over it. What moves shows them: water, falling water, foam, the grass that answers the wind, and each lamp's reach.

The engine is examples/island/live/live.js; what is water and what is grass is read from the art by analyse.js; the stills are made in Node by film.mjs. The art is art/island.png. Everything that moves is the almanac: the hour's tables, the cloud shadows, the lightning, the noise under the water, and the pixel shading.
